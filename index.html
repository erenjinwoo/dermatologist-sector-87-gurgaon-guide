Common Reasons to Consult a Dermatologist

People seek dermatology consultations for many different reasons. Some common concerns include:

  • Persistent or recurring acne
  • Acne marks and pigmentation
  • Uneven skin tone or texture
  • Hair loss and hair-related concerns
  • Signs of skin ageing
  • Unwanted hair
  • Skin conditions requiring medical assessment
  • Concerns about scars or changes in the skin

The appropriate treatment depends on the underlying cause and the individual's skin type, medical history, and goals.

Questions to Ask During Your Dermatology Consultation

Preparing a few questions before your appointment can help you make better use of your consultation. You could ask:

  • What could be causing my skin or hair concern?
  • What treatment options are available?
  • Are there alternatives to the recommended treatment?
  • How long might treatment take?
  • What kind of follow-up may be required?
  • Are there potential side effects or downtime?
  • What skincare or lifestyle changes could support the treatment?

How to Prepare for Your First Appointment

Before visiting a dermatologist, make a note of when the problem started, whether it has changed over time, and any treatments or skincare products you have already tried.

It can also be useful to bring information about medications or supplements you currently use. If your concern has changed noticeably over time, photographs showing the progression may also help during the consultation.

Medical Dermatology vs. Cosmetic Skin Treatments

Dermatology includes both medical care and cosmetic or aesthetic treatments. Medical dermatology focuses on diagnosing and managing skin, hair, and nail conditions, while aesthetic treatments may address concerns such as pigmentation, unwanted hair, skin texture, or signs of ageing.

Understanding the difference can help you communicate your goals clearly when arranging an appointment.
